# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 21 October 2026

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom

This Privacy Policy explains how Waffle ("Operator", "we", "us", "our", or "wafflerdot") collects, uses, processes, and safeguards personal data through the operation of the **ChiefXD Art Discord Bot** ("Bot", "Service").

---

## 1. Data Controller and Scope

* **Data Controller:** For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018), the data controller responsible for the operation of the Bot is Waffle (wafflerdot).


* **Scope of Application:** This policy applies to all individuals who interact with the Bot via slash commands, as well as users posting media in Discord channels actively monitored by the Bot (notably within the Art channel of the Chief's Crib server).



---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

We only process data strictly necessary to deliver moderation and image analysis functionality:

* **Discord Identifiers:** Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs to manage permissions, execute commands, and route responses.


* **Role and Permission Data:** Role IDs assigned to the bot's moderator whitelist and threshold management systems.


* **Media Content & URLs:** Image URLs, attachment links, and media metadata submitted via slash commands or posted in monitored channels.


* **Configuration & Audit Logs:** Per-guild threshold settings, history of threshold adjustments, and command invocation timestamps.



---

## 3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

Under Article 6 of the UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

* **Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)):** Processing message attachments, media URLs, and Discord IDs to maintain community safety, enforce server moderation rules, detect automated/AI-generated imagery, and prevent policy violations.


* **Performance of a Contract / Request (Article 6(1)(b)):** Processing commands directly invoked by users (such as `/analyse`, `/ai`, or `/reverse`) to return the requested analysis or search result.



---

## 4. Automated Screening (Limited Scope)

* **Designated Channel Ingestion:** In the "Chief's Crib" Discord server (`Guild ID: 800047499904679936`), and only in the Chief's Crib Discord server, the Bot automatically inspects all media submitted to the designated Art channel (`Channel ID: 818805988311302166`).


* **Automated Decision-Making & Profiling:** Submitted images are programmatically scanned to assess the likelihood of AI generation and to evaluate content against guild thresholds.


* **Human Oversight:** Scores and verdicts generated by the Bot serve as algorithmic moderation aids. Final disciplinary decisions (such as server bans or mutes) remain under the discretion and control of human guild administrators.



---

## 5. Third-Parties and International Transfers

To perform technical analysis, the Bot securely transmits image URLs to external third-party services:

* **Sightengine:** Image URLs are transmitted to Sightengine API endpoints to compute probabilistic scores for artificial intelligence generation, suggestive nudity, explicit nudity, and offensive symbols.


* **Reverse Image Search API:** When executing reverse lookups (`/reverse`), image URLs are sent to `google-reverse-image-api` endpoints to identify matching or visually similar web sources.


* **Cloud Infrastructure:** The Bot runtime and database instances (PostgreSQL/MySQL) are hosted on Google Cloud and managed database providers.



Where data transfers occur outside the United Kingdom, we ensure appropriate safeguards (such as UK International Data Transfer Agreements, Standard Contractual Clauses, or Adequacy Regulations) are maintained by our service providers.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Security

* **Ephemeral Image Processing:** Image URLs and attachment links are held in memory only for the duration required to complete the analysis request and are not permanently archived by the Bot.


* **Persistent Settings:** Guild threshold configurations, role whitelists, and threshold modification history are stored in a persistent database until manually removed or until the Bot leaves the server.


* **Technical Safeguards:** All API credentials, database strings, and Discord tokens are managed via encrypted secret managers and containerised deployment controls.



---

## 7. Your Rights

If you are a resident of the United Kingdom, you possess enforceable statutory rights regarding your personal data:

* **Right of Access:** You may request confirmation of whether we hold personal data relating to you and request a copy of that data.


* **Right to Rectification:** You may request the correction of inaccurate personal data.


* **Right to Erasure:** You may request the deletion of your Discord User ID and associated configuration records from our databases.


* **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** You have the right to submit a complaint regarding our data handling practices directly to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** via their official website at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).



---

## 8. Contacting Us

To exercise any of your data protection rights or request manual removal of your stored server configurations and permissions, please contact the bot operator and data controller via Discord (`wafflerdot`). Requests will be addressed without undue delay and within one calendar month in accordance with statutory requirements.
